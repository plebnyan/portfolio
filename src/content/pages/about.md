Six years in, the work rhymes. Data arrives somewhere inconvenient, four teams need it in four shapes, and nobody agrees on what a customer is. The tooling is never the hard part — the modelling is, and the discipline to keep raw and modelled data from bleeding into each other.

## How I work

**Model first, tool second.** A clean dimensional model outlives the platform it was built on. I've migrated the same star schema across three engines; I've never migrated a pipeline that skipped the modelling.

**Tests are part of the pipeline, not a phase after it.** Contracts on every model, failures that page someone, and a bronze layer nobody is allowed to edit. Trust is the actual deliverable — a warehouse people don't believe is just expensive storage.

**Write it down while it's still fresh.** The reasoning behind a decision decays faster than the code implementing it. That's most of why this site exists.

## What this site is

Two halves that point at each other. [Projects](/projects) are full case studies — the problem, the model, the decisions I'd defend, and the ones I'd take back. They're deliberately undated: I revise them as the work changes, so a date would only ever be wrong.

[The blog](/blog) is chronological and looser — what I'm learning, what broke, what I got wrong in public. Posts link into the projects they came out of, and every page lists what links back to it, so a thread can be followed in either direction instead of only forwards.
