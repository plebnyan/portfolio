Terminal monogram — variations

All riffs on concept #2: mono type, ink + green, a command-line feel. The cursors blink, like a real terminal. Same six letters of DNA, different personality.

pick one (or say "8 but the cursor from 2") and I'll finalise it + build the favicon/avatar/signature set.

“nlt” with a solid green cursor block after it. Clean, confident.
